A JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

The History of Puducherry

Puducherry has a rich and fascinating history that dates back several centuries. It was once an important trading centre where merchants from different parts of the world visited for business and cultural exchange.

The French established their settlement in Puducherry in 1674. Over the years, the city developed into an important French colonial centre in India. Its architecture, culture and lifestyle still reflect French traditions.

ONE UNION TERRITORY

Different Places.
One Identity.

The Union Territory of Puducherry is made up of four geographically separated regions. Each region is surrounded by a different neighbouring state, giving it a unique blend of culture, language, traditions and landscapes.

From the French-inspired streets of Puducherry to the peaceful rivers of Yanam, every region tells a different story.
