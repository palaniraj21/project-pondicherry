01 / ABOUT KARAIKAL

A Coastal Town With A Rich Heritage

Karaikal is a coastal region of the Union Territory of Puducherry, surrounded by Tamil Nadu. It is known for its ancient temples, peaceful beaches, traditional culture and rich heritage.
